Before complex systems, there are first parts, first assemblies and the first time geometry starts making mechanical sense.

Main Assembly and Kicker Insert are early projects from my Fusion 360 learning path. I keep them in the portfolio because progress is more interesting when the beginning is visible too.

01 / Main Assembly

One of my first Fusion 360 assembly projects.

Working with an assembly helped me start thinking about components as a system: how individual pieces relate in space, how structure is organized and how CAD becomes more than a single isolated shape.

This model marks the point where I started thinking spatially about relationships between components rather than treating 3D modeling as only surface creation.

The site presents the real GLB as an interactive object so the project can be inspected instead of reduced to one screenshot.

02 / Kicker Insert

An early individual-part study in Fusion 360.

Smaller components are useful because they force attention onto proportions, dimensions, fit and the idea that a part exists inside a larger mechanism.
