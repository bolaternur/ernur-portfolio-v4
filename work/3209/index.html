Let the object
do the talking.

This study is about viewpoint. Looking at the same object from a different angle reveals proportions and relationships that a single image can hide.

The model is presented as if it were on an engineering inspection table. The interface stays restrained so the mechanical form remains the focus.

The idea

A new view changes
what you notice.

  1. Silhouette

    Read the overall shape before focusing on a component.

  2. Proportion

    Compare the scale of different parts within the assembly.

  3. Alignment

    Follow how pieces fit together across a shared direction.

  4. Structure

    Connect the visible form with its mechanical relationships.
