This page deliberately uses a different 3D language from DECODE. No repeated explode trick. The interaction is about silhouette, rotation and camera compression.

As the field of view narrows, the scene approaches a technical inspection feel without fully switching away from perspective rendering.

Why two 3D languages?Consistency without repetition

Same portfolio.
Different rhythm.

DECODE teaches through separation. 3209 teaches through viewpoint. Giving each model its own behavior makes the site feel directed rather than decorated.

The common system is still the same: restrained typography, technical metadata, one motion curve and a camera that responds to the story.
