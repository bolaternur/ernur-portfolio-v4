Conceptual product flow

The work survives.
So should the reasoning.

Engineering teams create evidence constantly: code, CAD, photos, tests and observations. The hard part is remembering why something changed and which decision followed.

TraceLab connects those pieces into a history that can support engineering notebooks, coach insight, contribution evidence and handoff to the next season.

Capture the context

A file shows what changed. Capture adds the human reasoning around it, keeping the source and the student's contribution connected.

Keep the thread

Iterations lead to tests, tests inform decisions, and decisions carry forward into the next version of the work.

Inside the system

A place for the whole
engineering process.

The system includes team roles, seasons and subsystems, append-only evidence, versioned annotations, iterations, tests, decisions, offline capture and evidence triage.

Evidence Graph, Time Machine, Failure Library, search and handoff help the team navigate its history without losing unresolved questions or failed approaches.

Relationships
you can read.

The Evidence Trace is a bounded, read-only view of stored relationships. It connects source events, iterations, tests and decisions without inventing links between them.

Desktop uses React Flow with semantic edges and layered placement from ELK. On smaller screens, focused evidence cards keep the history readable.

Source first.
Always.

Student authorship and source provenance stay distinct. AI is contextual and linked to source material, with policy controls and an action log.

Privacy-aware capture, tenant boundaries and provenance-preserving exports are part of the system's design. The goal is a useful engineering memory that teams can trust.
