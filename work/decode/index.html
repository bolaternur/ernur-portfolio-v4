Drag to inspect. Rotate, separate or rebuild.

The original CAD assembly, presented as an interactive model.

Use the controls to rotate, separate and rebuild.

More than
a rotating object.

I wanted the model itself to explain how it is built. The original CAD hierarchy makes it possible to inspect components as parts of a complete machine.

Each part keeps its original position. Separation moves components outward from the robot's center, and reset returns the assembly to its starting state.

  1. Inspect

    Start with the complete machine and understand its overall form.

  2. Separate

    Move the parts apart to reveal the structure inside.

  3. Understand

    Follow the connections between drive, feedback and chassis.

  4. Rebuild

    Return every component to its place in the assembly.

Inside the machine

Every part has
a purpose.

Movement & feedback

Motors, gearboxes, wheels, shafts and encoder components make the relationship between motion and sensing visible.

Structure & mechanism

Plates, mounts, chassis and intake elements show how individual components become one working system.

Software meets
mechanics.

Robotics brings together several of my interests: programming, CAD, physical assembly and the feedback loop between an idea and a mechanism.

I want to keep developing both sides, improving mechanical design while going deeper into software control and competition robotics.
