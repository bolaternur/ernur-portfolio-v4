Real people.

During the summer, I designed, developed and sold websites to educational organizations, including businesses preparing students for admission to NIS, RFMSH and KTL.

That experience connected frontend work with content, communication and business. A website has to explain something clearly and make sense to people who did not build it.

From idea to interface

The whole process
belongs together.

  1. Understand

    Learn what the organization needs to communicate and who it serves.

  2. Structure

    Give the content a clear order so people can find what matters.

  3. Build

    Develop a responsive interface and refine how it behaves.

  4. Deliver

    Present the work to the client and connect design with practical value.

A clear interface
earns attention.

I care about responsive layouts, purposeful motion and visual identity. A mobile visitor deserves a considered experience, and a heavy visual idea still has to load and respond well.

I want to keep combining frontend engineering with 3D, interaction and strong project content, giving every choice a reason to be there.
